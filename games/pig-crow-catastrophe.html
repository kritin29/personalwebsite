P.I.G.: Crow Catastrophe

A third-person farm shooter: a gun-toting pig fights scarecrow-infected pigs, then the Infected Scarecrow itself.

Role
Sole programmer, team of 3
Engine
Unreal Engine 5.6, Blueprints
Jam
Oink Jam 2026
Built
4 days
> Play on itch.io

No generative AI was used. Some screenshots are from in-progress builds during the jam.

01The game

You play a pig with a gun on a farm overrun by pigs infected by an evil scarecrow. Clear the waves, trade up through pistols, SMGs, rifles, and snipers, then take down the fireball-throwing Infected Scarecrow.

02Core loop

  1. SpawnA farm arena, pistol in hand
  2. FightInfected pigs pour from spawners
  3. LootGrab dropped guns and health
  4. UpgradeSwap to SMG, rifle, or sniper
  5. BossBeat the Infected Scarecrow

04Systems

  • Weapon framework: a base weapon class with fire rate, damage, and hit effects; four fire-mode archetypes (full-auto, SMG, semi-auto pistol, sniper) and seven variants, each with a matching pickup and a shared interaction interface.
  • Player controller: Enhanced Input with eight actions; aiming drives a camera-boom and FOV timeline and slows movement; firing loops on a timer while held.
  • Enemy AI: a Behavior Tree with a roam task and a locate-player service; pawn sensing, timed contact damage, and pig squeals.
  • Boss AI: AI Perception with sight and damage senses, plus fireball and growing-shockwave attacks and its own health bar.
  • Game state: the game mode owns spawn caps, kill counts, and pickup budgets, feeding the HUD and the spawners.
  • UI: HUD, player and boss health, main menu, pause, and a game-over screen that restarts the level.

  • Farm arenaSilos, windmills, a water tower, a farmhouse, and a lava ring around the boss.
