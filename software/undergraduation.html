01Problem

Families juggle college lists, essays, activities, deadlines, and financial-aid paperwork across scattered tools. Parents can't see progress without asking, and students struggle to fit their experience into the Common App's tight formats.

02Solution

Two linked portals on one Supabase backend. Students build their college list, essays, and activities with Gemini's help; parents get a live view of that work plus their own financial-aid planner, document vault, and shared calendar.

03My part

I was the only developer on the student and parent portals and wrote every commit, from the Supabase schema and security policies to the Gemini prompts.

04How it works

  1. OnboardGrade, scores, majors, budget, regions
  2. RetrieveCollege data from a Pinecone index
  3. MatchWeighted score: major, budget, region, SAT, size
  4. CounselGemini answers with the page as context
  5. SyncParent portal updates live via Supabase Realtime
  • A parent account links to a student; six Supabase Realtime channels refresh the parent view whenever the student's colleges, essays, activities, calendar, or profile change.
  • Row-level security with a security-definer helper lets parents read their student's rows without recursive policies.
  • College data lives in a Pinecone index, with tuition, acceptance rate, test ranges, majors, and deadlines on each record; matches are ranked by a weighted score.
  • Gemini powers the counselor, an essay writer, editor, and grader (with an AI-likelihood score), and resume parsing into structured activities, with a rule-based fallback when no key is set.
  • Role-checked sign-in keeps students and parents in their own portals.
