The Strategist

A model that predicts whether a candidate will accept a job offer, plus a reporting tool.

Role
Data Science Intern
Dates
Jun – Aug 2025
Location
Hyderabad, India
Stack
Python · scikit-learn · Streamlit

The code and data are proprietary, so they aren't shown here.

01Problem

The team extended offers without a way to tell which candidates were likely to accept, and Power BI reports only broke results down one variable at a time.

02Solution

A classification model that predicts offer acceptance, and a Streamlit tool that turns single-variable Power BI reports into multi-variable Excel outputs.

03How it works

  1. InputCandidate data
  2. Modelscikit-learn classifier
  3. OutputOffer-acceptance prediction
  • Trained and evaluated a scikit-learn classifier on candidate data.
  • Built a Streamlit app that converts single-variable Power BI reports into multi-variable Excel outputs.

04Results

75%prediction accuracy

Takeaway

A model the team could understand and act on mattered more than squeezing out extra accuracy.
