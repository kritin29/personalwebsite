Ascend and Capture

Jetpack up a 7,500-stud World Tree, catch flying creatures with a rhythm minigame, and grow passive income at your base.

01The game

Players fly up through six stacked biome chambers, catching rarer creatures the higher they climb, then bank them at a home base that earns coins even offline. Coins buy jetpack tiers that unlock the next biome; eggs, trading, mounts, quests, and seasons sit on top.

04Systems

  • Cheat-resistant catching: the minigame runs on the client, so the server times each attempt and rejects catches faster than the creature's rarity allows.
  • Flight tuned to the world: each jetpack tier's reachable height maps to exactly one biome, and the glider auto-deploys when fuel runs out.
  • Mount riding with sphere-swept collision, which stopped animated creatures from dragging riders through walls.
  • Saves built for production: session locking, six versioned migrations, and purchases that can't be granted twice.
  • Safe trading: two-step confirmation, an offer signature against bait-and-switch, and server re-validation at the moment of trade.
  • A living ecosystem: per-biome population targets, weighted rarities, and weather that drives creature mutations.
