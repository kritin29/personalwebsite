01The game

A paleontology student brushes a fossil, finds a missing texture underneath, and wakes in build 0.66: the cut version of Earth where the dinosaurs never went extinct. With no weapons, he crosses one valley over three days by hiding, reading the wind, and throwing stones, while the raptors learn from how he survived the day before.

02Core loop

  1. ArriveEach day opens with a cinematic
  2. ObserveWatch animals, write field notes
  3. SneakCover, crouching, wind, and thrown stones
  4. SurviveEscape the day's chase
  5. Reach the seamGlitch light ends the day
  6. LearnA card names what they learned

04Systems

  • Cross-day learning AI: a subsystem that survives level loads records how you got through each day (time in ferns, logs, or water; stones thrown; distance sprinted). The next day, raptors search that cover first, trace stones to where they were thrown from, or split up to flank.
  • Raptor brain: sight and hearing perception plus a custom downwind smell check fuse into one awareness value that drives a six-state machine: patrol, suspicious, investigate, hunt, search, caught.
  • Stealth model: wind, weather, and cover volumes. Crouching in cover cuts visibility to about 4 m, and footsteps carry 2, 6, or 15 m depending on how you move.
  • Data-driven days: a director runs each day from structured steps, milestones, and cinematics, and checkpoints restore every hunter and herd exactly. A scripted player replays the game to test them.
  • Set pieces: a T-rex chase down a 300 m gully, a stampede of 960 instanced animals where the nearest swap to full skeletal meshes, and a Triceratops ride.
  • Shipping: cooked under a 1 GB cap on a 16 GB laptop, running at 99 fps at 720p on the heaviest day.

05Mechanics 16

  • Fossil dig prologueKneel and brush the dust off a fossil with the mouse.
  • Three-day valleyDay 1, Day 2, and a Final Day, then an epilogue and credits.
  • Hide anywhereCrouch in ferns, hollow logs, or water.
  • Adaptive raptorsThey change how they search, ignore distractions, and flank, based on your last day.
  • Thrown stonesDistract hunters, until they learn to trace the throw.
  • Read the windA pinch of dust shows the wind, and so where your scent is going.
  • Field notesObserve animals to fill an illustrated notebook that carries across days.
  • Developer's notesCollectibles written as fragments of the world's source code.
  • Herds and flocksHerds hide your scent and give moving cover; startled flocks give you away.
  • T-rex ambushA gully chase and a hollow-log scare on Day 2.
  • A world coming apartTears in the air, missing ground, meteor falls, and a stampede.
  • Triceratops rideBalance and grip your way through the finale.
  • Diegetic guidanceOne objective at a time and a walking waypoint, no clutter.
  • Voiced storyA voiced protagonist with positional audio throughout.
  • CheckpointsGetting caught restores the whole moment, not just your position.
  • Saves and menusMain menu, continue, restart the day, and saves at each day.
