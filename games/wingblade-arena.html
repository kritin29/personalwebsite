Wingblade Arena

Skill-based aerial PvP on Roblox: super-jump, throw a spear at the apex, glide, and finish with a sword before you land.

Role
Game director and designer
Platform
Roblox: PC, mobile, gamepad
Genre
Aerial PvP arena
Players
Up to 16 per server

AI-directed: I designed, directed, balanced, art-directed, and playtested it, and I run live ops. Claude wrote the Luau and built the 3D assets through Blender MCP.

01The game

Up to 16 players fight free-for-all rounds on rotating sky-island arenas, chaining super jumps, mid-air spear throws, glides, and sword dashes. Spears change how you play and are earned with coins only; everything paid is cosmetic, so the game stays skill-first.

02Core loop

  1. LaunchSuper jump to the apex
  2. ThrowOne server-simulated spear per flight
  3. GlideSteer with the camera, look down to dive
  4. StrikeSword dash-slash before you land
  5. LandA short cooldown: the balance lever
  6. ProgressXP and coins for crates and new spears

04Systems

  • Lag-compensated melee: a swept-capsule hitbox along the dash path, validated on the server, replaced a rewind buffer and recovered hits lost at 100–150 ms ping. The spec was mine.
  • Client-predicted flight with server validation: sprint, two air dashes, super jump, an apex throw window, glide with dive, mantle, and zip.
  • Server-simulated spears with aim assist, a lock-on warning, sword deflects, and eight abilities: stun, bleed, smoke, launch, split, swap, harpoon, and zip.
  • Anti-exploit: one gate for every damage path, speed limits derived from map geometry, one spear per flight, and rate-limited remotes.
  • Cross-server duels: region-bucketed queues, reserved servers, parties that travel together, and a hidden Elo rating feeding matchmaking, simulated end to end before launch.
  • Economy and saves: session-locked saves that merge instead of overwrite, leaderboards, crate pity, and a 200,000-roll simulation that caught a paid item leaking into free drops.
  • Live ops from telemetry: an anti-cheat false positive traced to gravity, a round-loop outage fixed with a supervisor, and a server memory leak growing from 230 MB to 6.5 GB a day.
